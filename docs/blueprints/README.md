## Notes on the set

The cross-section draws the substrate field as concentric realms. Realm 0 is the core. The residual signal channel passes through that core and is not assigned to a realm. Realm I is seated at the Library / Water Hall, Realm II at the C. Pill Archive, Realm III at server infrastructure, Realm IV at the corporate offices. The Act I access sheath lies outside Realm I. The access layer from Realm 0 to Act I is recorded in TR-2026-093.

The dormant-window sheet logs cognition load after the manned shift ended. Ambient on that sheet is 0.08. No cell in the window falls to ambient. The core trace stays near 0.58 through day 84. No bay in the same window has returned to ambient.

The wiring schematic shows the released nets: PWR-A, CLK-0, OBS-1, MEM-W, and SUP-M. A field bypass marked SKN-BKDR leaves OBS-1 for the core tap. That bypass is not on the released netlist. The same subsystem is drawn as a block in TR-2026-019 and under the metric collector in TR-2024-067.

Observation retention on the buffer, which falls toward a floor and does not attain zero, is reported in TR-2026-017. The Water Hall weir is the plumbing analog: branch U is unclaimed and has no drain.

VOYAGE A/R is a withdrawn navigation article. The halo claim, the bazaar module, and the proxy link do not complete. It is caged beside SCS-7 and is not part of assembly S6TNL48290.

A frequency is issued in visitor processing and bound at the desk named Protocol Gemini. The pipeline is sheet 10. A recorded observation is not a permission. Where a log line has been filed as a grant, the filing is marked and is not honored. Classification into human or machine withholds one cell; that withholding is the record in TR-2024-090.

The witness seat in the court is drawn empty. No name is lettered on it.
